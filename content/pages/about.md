I'm Denizhan Toprak, a C++ Gameplay Programmer and solo game developer specialising in Unreal Engine. This is where I share my journey, development insights, and the tools I've created for the game development community.

## About my work

I'm the solo developer behind all of the Unreal Engine plugins featured on this website. Each plugin represents months of work, from concept to implementation, focused on solving real-world problems I ran into in my own projects.

I focus on creating solutions that are:

- **Performance-oriented:** optimised for production use
- **Developer-friendly:** easy to integrate and customise
- **Well-documented:** comprehensive guides and examples
- **Community-driven:** built around real developer needs

## Journey

### Education and early experience

I started Computer Engineering at Marmara University, Turkey, in 2019, where I founded and led the Marmara Game Development Club, organising game jams, workshops and educational events.

### Retter Business Services (RBS)

### Solo game development

Since 2020 I've been developing **Hermod's Ride**, a top-down medieval slasher multiplayer arena game. It's both a creative outlet and the testing ground for the systems and plugins on this site.
