The site has a new home. The portfolio, the dev blog and all plugin documentation now live in one place, built with Flutter.

<!-- truncate -->

## Why rebuild?

The old site did its job, but writing for it felt like maintaining a web project instead of writing. The new site keeps the parts that worked:

- **Markdown first.** Every blog post and documentation page is a plain `.md` file.
- **Docusaurus-style docs.** A sidebar, a table of contents and previous/next links.
- **One place for everything.** Plugins, devlogs and contact links share a single home.
